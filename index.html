About Squeaky Clean Floors

Care for every surface. Pride in every detail.

We provide professional carpet, tile, and floor care services designed to keep your surfaces clean, fresh, and well-maintained. Our team uses safe and effective cleaning methods to remove dirt, stains, and buildup from all types of flooring.

We focus on restoring the original shine and improving the overall appearance of your floors. Every service is delivered with care, attention to detail, and reliable results—with the goal of supporting a cleaner, healthier indoor environment.
